# Data inventory (DPDP, PR-1 to PR-14)

What personal data the platform holds, why, how long, and how each right applies. The
spec's data inventory is the source of truth; this file maps it to tables and code. Adding a
new personal-data field means updating this file and the privacy notice first (CLAUDE.md §8).

**Rights in code:** export `services/privacy` `exportMyData` (PRV-2) · deletion
`deleteMyAccount` → `finaliseDeletions` → `anonymiseUser` (PRV-4) · corrections
`requestCorrection` (PRV-3) · retention jobs in `worker/jobs/{retention,privacy}.ts` (PR-9).

| Data (table)                                                               | Whose             | Why                                | Kept until                                                              | Export                                 | On deletion                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Name, email, password hash (`User`)                                        | All               | Account and login                  | Deletion; unverified sign-ups 7 days; inactive 3 years (warned)         | Yes                                    | Anonymised: "Deleted user", placeholder email, no password                            |
| `User.canonicalEmail`                                                      | Brand owners      | One trial per company (UC-B1)      | Deletion                                                                | No (derived)                           | Removed                                                                               |
| Consent records (`ConsentRecord`)                                          | All               | Proof of consent (PR-3)            | Deletion + legal hold (kept, linked to the anonymised row)              | Yes                                    | Kept for the legal hold                                                               |
| Sessions (`Session`)                                                       | All               | Sign-in, "sign out everywhere"     | 30-day sliding expiry; purged daily                                     | Yes (device, IP, times)                | Deleted                                                                               |
| One-time links, Google link (`OneTimeToken`, `AuthAccount`)                | All               | Verification, resets, Google login | Expiry; purged daily                                                    | No                                     | Deleted                                                                               |
| Security and admin logs (`AuditEvent`)                                     | All               | Security, fraud, accountability    | 1 year (SR-13)                                                          | No                                     | Kept until the 1-year expiry (PRV-4)                                                  |
| Creator profile, languages, categories, rate card, portfolio, photo        | Creators          | Profile and search                 | Deletion or removal                                                     | Yes                                    | Anonymised profile; lists, rate card, portfolio and photo deleted                     |
| Social accounts, encrypted tokens, snapshots, audience (`SocialAccount` …) | Creators          | Stats, score, verification         | Disconnect or deletion; daily snapshots older than 2 years kept monthly | Yes (no tokens)                        | Deleted (with snapshots and audience)                                                 |
| Scores, authenticity flags (`ScoreResult`, `AuthenticityFlag`)             | Creators          | Score (FR-4)                       | Deletion                                                                | Scores                                 | Deleted                                                                               |
| Brand, team membership, invites (`Brand`, `BrandMember`, `BrandInvite`)    | Brands            | Brand account and team             | Deletion of the last member                                             | Yes                                    | Member removed; last member: brand renamed "Deleted brand", lists and invites deleted |
| Shortlists, notes, saved searches, profile views                           | Brands            | Discovery tools                    | Deletion                                                                | Yes                                    | Private lists and saved searches deleted; team-shared lists stay with the team        |
| Requests, chats, attachments, deals, posts, ratings                        | Both              | Collaboration                      | Deletion of both sides                                                  | Requests, own messages, deals, ratings | Other side keeps its copy; the deleted person shows as "Deleted user"                 |
| Reports (`Report`)                                                         | Reporter          | Moderation                         | Deletion (reporter link removed)                                        | Yes                                    | Reporter link removed; report kept for moderation history                             |
| Notifications, preferences                                                 | All               | FR-12                              | 180 days                                                                | Yes                                    | Deleted                                                                               |
| Credit packs (`CreditPack`)                                                | Brands            | Pay-as-you-go purchases            | With the brand                                                          | No                                     | Kept with the brand record                                                            |
| Data requests (`DataRequest`)                                              | All               | Track rights requests (PR-5)       | Kept as a record of compliance                                          | Yes                                    | Kept (linked to the anonymised row)                                                   |
| Email outbox (`EmailOutbox`)                                               | All               | Sending email                      | 30 days after sending, payload cleared at once                          | No                                     | Deleted                                                                               |
| Rate-limit counters                                                        | All (hashed keys) | Abuse protection                   | 1 day                                                                   | No                                     | Expire                                                                                |

**Not collected:** Aadhaar, PAN, date of birth, home address, bank or UPI details, social
media passwords, device contacts or location, phone numbers (Q-15).
