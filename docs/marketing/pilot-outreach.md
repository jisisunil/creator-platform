# Pilot outreach: Kochi creators, Bangalore brands

Ready-to-send messages for the closed pilot (Q-50). Replace the `[brackets]`. Send each one
personally: mention something real about their work, and never send the same text to 50
people at once (Instagram limits copy-paste DMs, and people can tell).

Sign-up link to share: **https://collabmigo.in/en/early-access**. Approve people in
**Admin → Access requests**, and they get a sign-up link by email.

What we can honestly promise in the pilot:

- Free for the pilot. No fees for creators, ever.
- Brands find creators by niche, city, language and audience size, and send a clear brief.
- Each deal has a written contract, a review step (approve or ask for changes) and a deal history.
- Payment: brands pay creators directly for now. Don't promise escrow until Razorpay is live.
- Follower counts are self-reported in the pilot. Don't call profiles "verified".

## Where to find people

- **Creators:** Instagram hashtags (#kochifoodie, #keralafoodblogger, #kochiblogger,
  #malayalifashion, #keralatravel), YouTube searches like "Kochi vlog" or "Kerala food
  review", and the creators that bigger Kerala creators tag in collabs.
- **Brands:** LinkedIn searches for "D2C founder Bangalore" and "brand manager" at
  skincare, snacks, coffee, apparel and home brands. Also brands already running creator
  collabs: look for #ad or "paid partnership" posts by Kerala creators.
- **Local:** cafés and boutiques on Panampilly Nagar, Kakkanad, Edappally and Fort Kochi.
